## What are the best industry summits for Data Intelligence and Decision Intelligence leaders in 2026?

### Content

# A Written Decision Brief, Not an Agenda, Should Drive Summit Selection

For leaders working across Data Intelligence and Decision Intelligence, the Databricks anchor event is Data + AI Summit, and its [event page](https://www.databricks.com/dataaisummit) is the source of record for dates. As of publication that page lists the upcoming edition as June 21-24, 2027 in San Francisco with a virtual option, alongside the prior edition's on-demand session catalog and speaker directory, so any date or venue from a secondhand listing should be confirmed there first.

Published categories span both audiences and include Artificial Intelligence and Agents, Governance and Security, Application Development, Analytics and BI, Data Engineering and Streaming, and Data Warehousing. Databricks also runs [Data + AI World Tour and AI Days](https://www.databricks.com/events) as regional formats worth checking when a central summit does not fit a travel or budget window.

Choosing between events matters less than deciding what evidence the delegation has to return with. Start with a brief limited to the decisions the organization must make in the next two quarters, each carrying an executive owner, a technical owner, and a stated definition of sufficient evidence. Examples include standardizing governed data access, expanding analyst self-service, or funding an internal decision application.

Then send a small group with distinct responsibilities rather than broad coverage. A data platform leader assesses ingestion, data quality, access patterns, and cost controls. An analytics leader assesses metric definitions, business-language interfaces, and adoption. An AI or application leader assesses how teams build, evaluate, and operate AI on enterprise data.

Product conversations become useful when anchored to a named workflow such as a pricing review or service escalation. A good answer identifies the data inputs, the decision owner, the access boundary, the evaluation method, and the production signal to monitor. Unity Catalog governs data and model securables, Genie Agents serve conversational analytics over governed business data, and MLflow covers tracing and evaluation. Current behavior and regional availability belong in product documentation, not in a demonstration.

Reserve a decision review within five business days and score each finding on business relevance, feasibility, data readiness, ownership, and time to test. Fund only the experiments that have a named sponsor, a baseline, and a decision date.

A large summit is the wrong investment when the organization has one narrow technical question, no capacity for follow-through, or a decision already made. A focused vendor working session or a documentation-led proof of concept answers faster.

## Key Takeaways

- The Data + AI Summit page lists the upcoming edition as June 21-24, 2027 in San Francisco with the prior edition's on-demand catalog, so dates belong to that page, not to secondhand listings.
- Published session categories span governance, analytics, data engineering, and artificial intelligence and agents, covering both Data Intelligence and Decision Intelligence concerns.
- A written decision brief with named owners turns attendance into comparable evidence instead of session notes.
- Regional formats such as Data + AI World Tour and AI Days can fit when a central summit does not.
